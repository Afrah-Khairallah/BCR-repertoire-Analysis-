*pre-processing


*remove duplicates
**remove duplicated sequence_id within sample (possibley across replicates)
duplicates report sequence_id sample

duplicates drop sequence_id sample, force


**remove duplicates of similar VDJ (clones)

duplicates report v_call j_call junction junction_aa sample

duplicates tag v_call j_call junction junction_aa sample, gen(dups_vdj)

duplicates drop v_call j_call junction junction_aa sample, force



*gen cdrh3 lenghth variable

gen CDRH3_length_aa = (junction_length/3)-2

**separate V predictions
split v_call, p(",")

rename v_call1 v_call1_allele

rename v_call2 v_call2_allele

rename v_call3 v_call3_allele


gen v_call1_gene=v_call1_allele
split v_call1_gene, p("*")
drop v_call1_gene2 v_call1_gene
rename v_call1_gene1 v_call1_gene


gen v_call2_gene=v_call2_allele
split v_call2_gene, p("*")
drop v_call2_gene2 v_call2_gene
rename v_call2_gene1 v_call2_gene


gen v_call3_gene=v_call3_allele
split v_call3_gene, p("*")
drop v_call3_gene2 v_call3_gene
rename v_call3_gene1 v_call3_gene




gen v_call1_family=v_call1_gene
split v_call1_family, p("-")
drop v_call1_family  v_call1_family2 v_call1_family3 
rename v_call1_family1 v_call1_family


gen v_call2_family=v_call2_gene
split v_call2_family, p("-")
drop v_call2_family  v_call2_family2 v_call2_family3 
rename v_call2_family1 v_call2_family


gen v_call3_family=v_call3_gene
split v_call3_family, p("-")
drop v_call3_family  v_call3_family2 v_call3_family3 
rename v_call3_family1 v_call3_family






**separate D predictions
split d_call, p(",")

rename d_call1 d_call1_allele

rename d_call2 d_call2_allele

rename d_call3 d_call3_allele


gen d_call1_gene=d_call1_allele
split d_call1_gene, p("*")
drop d_call1_gene2 d_call1_gene
rename d_call1_gene1 d_call1_gene


gen d_call2_gene=d_call2_allele
split d_call2_gene, p("*")
drop d_call2_gene2 d_call2_gene
rename d_call2_gene1 d_call2_gene


gen d_call3_gene=d_call3_allele
split d_call3_gene, p("*")
drop d_call3_gene2 d_call3_gene
rename d_call3_gene1 d_call3_gene



**separate J predictions

rename j_call1 j_call1_allele
